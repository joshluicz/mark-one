# mark-one

Joshua Lui's hardware workshop. **Bench to Flight**: a logic probe, then an 8-bit CPU from discrete
logic, then bare-metal firmware — and finally a build aimed at a real problem, chosen once I have
the tools to choose well. Each stage closes on a physical object, documented as it happens.

I do the building and the research. My brother is my apprentice at the bench: he knows the plan, and his ideas get logged with his name on them. Claude (Anthropic) wrote the
syllabus and session guides and coaches along the way — credited wherever it contributed.
