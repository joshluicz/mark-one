# S0.7 — Soldering II: desoldering, and the permanent dimmer
**Module M0.3 · 🛠️ bench · ~3 h · objectives 0.3.5, 0.3.6, 0.3.7, 0.3.8 · closes M0.3**

## Before you start
- ⚠️ **Hazard:** same as S0.6 — eyes, iron, fumes. Same blocker rule.
- **Have:** desoldering braid, solder pump, perfboard, hookup wire (stranded for flying leads,
  solid for on-board links), 9 V clip, the S0.5 dimmer parts.
- **Write answers first:** Q0.3.c.

## Steps
1. **Desolder with braid** (0.3.5): lay braid on the joint, iron on top of the braid, wait for the
   solder to wick up, lift both together. Don't drag.
2. **Desolder with the pump:** heat the joint, trigger the pump right next to it, pull the part.
   Remove three resistors from S0.6's board with each method. If you lift a pad, that's Q0.3.c in
   real life — log it.
3. **Plan the layout on paper first** (0.3.6). Draw the perfboard as a grid. Place the battery
   clip, the selector, four resistors, the LED. Decide where + and − run. **No soldering until the
   drawing is done.**
4. **Wire gauge** (0.3.8). Your currents are milliamps — thin hookup wire is fine. Write the
   reasoning in one line.
5. **Build it.** Components in, bend leads to hold, solder, trim.
6. **Strain relief** (0.3.7). The battery clip's wires will flex. Anchor them so the flex doesn't
   load the solder joint — loop them through a spare hole, or a dab of hot glue / a cable tie.
7. **Test every position** against the S0.5 prediction sheet.

## Done when — closes M0.3
The syllabus pass criterion: **the perfboard build survives being dropped onto a desk from waist
height and still works.** Drop it. Film the drop. → Build-log entry marking **M0.3 closed**.
